Meet our providers

Credentialed care. Every visit.

Three clinicians. Board certifications in dermatology, family practice, and Mohs-trained surgical expertise — the medical depth behind Skin Fitness.

Rachel Cohen, NP-C, DCNP

Dermatology nurse practitioner · Creator of the Skin Fitness framework

Rachel, a dedicated family nurse practitioner, has nearly 20 years of experience caring for patients of all ages. In 2014 she pursued specialization in dermatology, achieving board certification through the Dermatology Nurse Practitioner Certification Board. She earned her BA from Boston University and her MSN from the MGH Institute of Health Professions.

Her practice is rooted in health promotion and disease prevention: helping patients attain optimal skin health through regular skin examinations and healthy skincare habits built into daily routines. She has a particular passion for non-surgical facial rejuvenation — daily active skincare, injectables, and advanced resurfacing procedures — and leads the Skin Fitness program.

  • Board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner (NP-C)
  • Dermatology Certified Nurse Practitioner (DCNP)
  • BA, Boston University · MSN, MGH Institute of Health Professions
  • Creator of the Skin Fitness skin-conditioning framework

Jay L. Cohen, MD, JD

Dermatologist · Founder

Dr. Cohen, a board-certified dermatologist, is a graduate of Cornell University and New Jersey Medical School. He completed his internship at Beth Israel Medical Center in New York and trained in dermatology at Columbia-Presbyterian Medical Center and the Boston University–Tufts joint program.

Before his residency, he engaged in research on nail growth and the treatment of fungal nail infections as part of a dermatopharmacology research fellowship at Columbia-Presbyterian — an interest he maintains, treating a wide variety of nail disorders. He holds hospital staff appointments at St. Elizabeth's Medical Center and Newton-Wellesley Hospital, where he is an active member of the inpatient consultation service and participates in the annual community-wide skin cancer screening. He is also a graduate of Boston University School of Law, admitted to the bar in New York and New Jersey.

  • Fellow, American Academy of Dermatology
  • Charter Member, Council on Nail Disorders
  • Massachusetts Academy of Dermatology · New England Dermatological Society
  • American Medical Association · Massachusetts Medical Society

Kerry Fike, MD, RPh

Dermatologist · Mohs surgeon

Dr. Fike is double board-certified in dermatology and micrographic dermatologic surgery. She is a graduate of Harvard Medical School and the Harvard Combined Dermatology Residency Training Program, with advanced Mohs surgery training at the Florida State University College of Medicine Micrographic Surgery & Dermatologic Oncology Fellowship at Dermatology Associates of Tallahassee.

She specializes in nonmelanoma skin cancer treatment, and also treats dysplastic nevi and other benign skin growths that may require dermatologic surgery.

  • Fellow, American Academy of Dermatology
  • Fellow, American Society for Dermatologic Surgery
  • Fellow, American College of Mohs Surgery

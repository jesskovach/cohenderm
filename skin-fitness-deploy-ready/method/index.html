The Skin Fitness method

Four phases. One year. Measurable skin health.

Skin Fitness is Rachel Cohen's framework — she created it, owns it, and leads it here. Published in the Journal of Clinical and Aesthetic Dermatology (2026). You train your body over time; your skin adapts the same way. Each phase builds on the last, moving from a strong barrier to conditioned, resilient skin you can measure.

I Foundation

Barrier repair and baseline imaging. Every plan starts with a full medical skin exam.

II Active Training

Conditioning. Retinoids and actives are introduced gradually and titrated — dose follows tolerance.

III Performance

Once your skin is conditioned, targeted in-office work — peels, lasers, injectables — lands better and lasts longer.

IV Maintenance

Measure the outcome. Re-image, compare to baseline, and set the maintenance plan. Progress you can see.

It begins the way every relationship with this practice begins — with a comprehensive medical exam.

The program — now in pilot

A structured year of skin conditioning — with a clinician, not a counter.

Begin with a Skin Fitness assessment. From there, Rachel maps your skin's condition and builds a staged plan through the four phases — with objective measurement at checkpoints 1, 2, and 3 months. Especially valuable in perimenopause, when skin changes fastest.

The program is entering pilot: a first group of participants, measured closely. Public applications open beginning January 2027.

  • Plan built and reviewed with a clinician, every phase
  • Regimens titrated to your skin, not a product quota
  • Progress measured against your own baseline imaging
  • Phase check-ins in person or virtual — your choice

Appointments

Booking a visit

Our office is open daily, Monday through Friday. New patients join by application — start online, or call 781-449-3588 and our staff will take your application by phone. Established patients schedule directly at the same number.

Arrive 15 minutes early for your first appointment
Prepare

What to bring

Fill out your registration and medical history forms ahead of time and bring them with you. Consulting about a skin rash? Bring a list of your current medications — prescription and nonprescription — and, if possible, the actual packaging of any topicals; the ingredient listings often help us make a diagnosis.

Telehealth

Virtual visits

Virtual visits are available for select concerns and follow-ups. Note your preference on your application, or ask when scheduling. Initial medical exams and skin cancer screenings are in person.

Proposed offering — see Services
Coverage

Insurance

If your insurance requires a referral for specialist visits, contact your primary care physician's office before your visit so the referral is in our office on the date of your appointment. Without one, you'll be asked to sign a waiver, as required by your insurer. We participate in most managed health care plans, and our billing service will submit claims for you. Copayments, deductibles, and payments for non-covered services — such as cosmetic procedures — are due at the time of service.

Your policy is a contract between you and your insurer
Urgent needs

Emergencies

We reserve several appointments each day for emergencies. In case of an emergency after business hours, Dr. Cohen can be reached by a telephone paging system.

Same-day time, every day
Policies

Cancellations & missed visits

Contact the office as soon as possible so we can offer your time to another patient. Repeated missed appointments, or cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, may result in a fee:

  • Medical appointment — $100
  • Surgical appointment — $200
  • Cosmetic consultation or appointment — $150
Arriving 15+ minutes late may require rescheduling

Medical dermatology

Skin cancer prevention

Early detection remains the best method for preventing skin cancer. Regardless of the reason for your visit, we recommend a comprehensive skin examination at your initial appointment — particularly if you've had extensive sun exposure or a family history of skin cancer. It's an opportunity for the doctor to check for suspicious moles and early skin cancers, especially in areas that are difficult to examine yourself.

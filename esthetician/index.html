Meet our esthetician

Marina Kitzis, Medical Esthetician.

Over thirty years in esthetics — facial rejuvenation and the treatment of acne, mild scarring, and pigmentary changes, for both teenagers and adults. Inside a medical dermatology practice.

Three decades of training, still adding to it

Marina trained in Moscow at the Institute of Esthetics, completing an intensive two-year program with an emphasis on nutrition and holistic methods. She has worked in the Newton area for more than twenty-five years, and has continued her education with specialty courses at the Catherine Hinds and Elizabeth Grady schools of esthetics.

Facials with a clinical backbone

Marina's focus is facial rejuvenation and the treatment of acne, mild scarring, and pigmentary changes, using glycolic peels and facials for both teenagers and adults. Facials — alone or in combination with glycolic acid peels — help maintain a healthy complexion, and can be an important part of a complete acne protocol, enhancing the effectiveness of both oral and topical acne medications.

What a facial involves

A thorough cleansing of the skin and pores, in which blackheads, whiteheads, and tiny cysts known as milia are precisely removed. Often a mask finishes the procedure — to tighten pores or rehydrate the skin.

Esthetician services

Facials · Glycolic acid peels · Teen and adult acne treatment · Extractions · Pigmentation and mild scarring · Daily skin-care regimen guidance

Privacy

Cookie policy.

What this website stores on your device, why, and how to change it. Last updated —.

The short version

This site does not sell your data, does not run advertising trackers, and does not attempt to identify you. It stores two small preferences on your device so the site behaves the way you left it.

What is stored

Name Purpose Kept
sf-themeRemembers whether you chose light or dark mode.Until cleared
sf-consentRemembers your choice below, so you are not asked again.12 months

Both are strictly necessary in the sense that they exist only to honor a preference you set. Neither is shared with anyone.

Analytics

Analytics are off unless you turn them on. If enabled, they measure page views and general traffic patterns in aggregate. They are never used to identify individuals, and are not used for advertising.

Analytics stay off on any page where you are signing in to Patient Gateway, requesting an appointment, or submitting an application, regardless of your choice.

Leaving this site

Some links take you elsewhere: Patient Gateway (Mass General Brigham), our payment processor, and downloadable patient forms. Once you follow one of those links you are on their site, under their privacy and cookie policies, not ours.

Health information

This website is not where your medical record lives. Information you enter into an application form is used to contact you and schedule care. Clinical questions, results, and photographs belong in Patient Gateway, which is the protected channel for protected health information.

Your choices

Change your decision at any time using the buttons here. You can also clear cookies in your browser settings, which resets everything on this site. This site honors Global Privacy Control signals.

Current setting: essential only

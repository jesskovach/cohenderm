Joining the practice

New patients begin with a Skin Fitness assessment.

The assessment is Rachel's starting point: she reads your skin, sets a baseline, and builds the plan. Tell us what brings you in, choose a provider, and share photos if you'd like — every application is read personally by the clinical team.

  1. Apply online. Two minutes. Photos optional.
  2. Personal review. Rachel and the clinical team read every application.
  3. Invitation — or your place in line. We respond by text or email to book your first visit. While the pilot is full, new applications join the waitlist in order received — and waitlisted applicants have first access when the program opens to the public.
  4. Initial medical exam. Every new patient begins with a comprehensive skin examination — the baseline for everything that follows.

Why an application? The Skin Fitness program is in pilot — structured intake keeps it small enough to measure. Public applications open beginning January 2027, and applying now places you on the waitlist, in order received. Medical concerns are prioritized in review, and we reserve time each day for urgent needs. How access works →

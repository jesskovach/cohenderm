About the practice

A medical practice, built for the long term.

Founded by Dr. Jay Cohen. Grown deliberately, one credential at a time. Independent then, independent now.

The comprehensive exam

Established in Needham

Dr. Cohen founded the practice and has provided medical dermatology to Needham and the surrounding communities for decades. He holds hospital staff appointments at St. Elizabeth's Medical Center and Newton-Wellesley Hospital, serves on the inpatient consultation service, and participates in the annual community-wide skin cancer screening.

Built deliberately

The practice added depth where it mattered. Dr. Kerry Fike brought double board certification in dermatology and micrographic dermatologic surgery. Rachel Cohen, NP-C, DCNP, brought two decades of patient care and a dermatology specialization. Marina Kitzis brought over thirty years of medical esthetics. Each addition answered a clinical need.

Independent

The practice is independent — every clinical decision is made in this office, by the clinicians whose names are on the door, at one address on Hillside Avenue.

How access works

Exclusive in attention. Not in access.

The application process exists for a reason: the Skin Fitness program is in pilot, and structured intake keeps it small enough to measure. Access stays open in every way that matters.

Online

Apply in two minutes

Every application is read personally by the clinical team. Invitations to schedule go out by text or email. While the pilot is full, applications join the waitlist in order received — with first access when the program opens to the public.

By phone

Call 781-449-3588

Our staff takes applications by phone, Monday through Friday. Established patients schedule directly — no application needed.

Emergencies

Same-day time, every day

We reserve appointments each day for emergencies. After business hours, Dr. Cohen can be reached by a telephone paging system.

Medical need

Medicine comes first

Medical concerns are prioritized in application review. A worrying mole is never in line behind a cosmetic consult.
